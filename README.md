sign up in the website 
To use the website and interact with other students, you first need to create an account or sign in.

Visit the Portal: When you open the website, you will see the Campus Connect Portal card right in the center of the screen.

Choose an Option:

Select Register Profile if you are a new user.

Select Sign In if you already have an account.

Fill in Your Details (For Registration):

Username: Enter a unique username.

Password: Create a secure password.

Department: Choose your academic department from the list (e.g., AIML, Data Science, Computer Science Core, Mechanical, Electrical, Sports Club, or Medical & Pharmacy).

Academic Year: Pick your current year (1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, or PG/PhD).

Pick Avatar: Choose a fun animal avatar (like a Fox, Panda, Lion, or Owl) that will represent you across events and chats.

Click "Create Campus Account": Once submitted, you will be logged in immediately and taken to the main events feed.

functions in the website
Once signed in, you have access to a variety of interactive campus features:

1. Browse and Filter Campus Events
View all upcoming campus meetups, workshops, tournaments, and summits in the live events feed.

Use the Department Filter dropdown at the top to view events specific to a certain department or club.

2. Join or Leave Event Rosters
Every event card shows details like date, time, venue, description, and the host.

Click Join Event to add your name and avatar to the attendee roster.

You can expand the attendee list to see who else is participating. If you need to back out, simply click Leave Event.

3. Host and Publish Your Own Events
Click the + Publish Event button at the top of the dashboard.

Fill out the event details, including the title, department, venue, and participant limit.

Use the interactive Calendar Date Picker to select the date.

Use the Analog Clock Picker (by clicking the clock dial or selecting hour/minute buttons) to set the exact time.

Click Publish Campus Event to make it instantly visible to everyone on the platform.

4. Chat with Other Students
Click the 💬 Chat button in the top navigation bar to open the messaging window.

On the left directory, you will see all registered students on the website.

Click on any student's name to open a direct conversation, send messages, and coordinate with peers in real-time.
